*The Global DH Symposium celebrated its 10th anniversary in 2025. This website and resource manual emerged from conversations around preserving the Symposium's institutional memory and ensuring its continuity. We're proud to share our experiences and know-how in organizing a truly global digital humanities symposium that actively centers marginalized communities in this field.*

---
## What is the *Resource Manual*?

The *Global DH Symposium Resource Manual* documents key information, procedures, and processes needed to run the Symposium. This public site serves as both a guide for future GDHS organizers and collaborators, and a resource for anyone interested in learning from our decade of experience.

The *Resource Manual* is also our way of building organizational memory. By writing down our processes and best practices, we make sure the Symposium doesn't depend on any one person's knowledge. This was, it can continue smoothly as organizers or even organizing institutions change. Our processes have been tested over the years and improved as we tried new approaches to collecting submissions or inviting reviewers. The *Resource Manual* offer insights into the motivations and the reasons behind these changes.

## Who is the *Resource Manual* for?

- **Advisory and Organizing Committee members**, both new and returning, who want a clear sense of how the symposium works and what their role involves.
- **Potential future co-hosts**, who can use it to understand our mission, vision, and values, and to see how we put those values into practice.
- Anyone interested in the GDHS

The *Resource Manual* is not our main website. For information about this year's Symposium, including the **Call for Papers** or **Symposium Schedule** please follow this [link](https://msuglobaldh.org/) Previous years' **Proceedings** can be found [here](https://msuglobaldh.org/past-symposia/).
